Usage at King & Bathurst and Richview. Managed appointments include cancellations, no-shows and already-created future bookings. Customers are approximate deduplicated booking contacts, including staff-assisted bookings. Groomers are enabled profiles with appointments. These figures are a dated snapshot.

The interesting part

What happens
after the happy path?

The small decisions are often the ones a working product depends on.

01Two people want the same slot.+

Availability checks need to hold when a booking is written. Firestore transactions enforce scheduling constraints across customer and staff booking flows.

02A payment event arrives twice.+

Stripe deposits use signature-verified webhooks, duplicate-booking safeguards and idempotent refunds to account for retries and booking conflicts.

03The right data, for the right store.+

Server-side membership, role and location checks scope access for customers, groomers and managers. Automated authorization-policy tests check those boundaries.
